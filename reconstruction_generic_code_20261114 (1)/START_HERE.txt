The hidden block leaves observed point projections unchanged and never drops
UVs/tracks. It improves the shape prior but does NOT remove all protrusions.
For coefficient 0.001, final raw point RMS is 0.710 px, mask IoUs are 98.315%
and 98.311%, depth/width is 1.4235, C8 surface RMS is 0.2010. There are zero
libigl intersections and zero first-hit violations. These are fitting and
validity measurements, not 3D accuracy against the real object.

Limits for the proposed paper: 3D remains visibly wrong in unseen regions;
no ground truth, metric scale, uniqueness or global-optimality certificate;
the two web images are not verified as one rigid capture. SIFT/mask fitting
does not match RGB or establish a visible-surface bijection. Topology is
fixed to a closed genus-zero mesh. C8 is assumed, not inferred as a fact.
This is NOT a DCA implementation or a trained neural reconstruction model.
Do not publish fitting IoU as 3D reconstruction accuracy.
